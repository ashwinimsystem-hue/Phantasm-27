About

Two decades of building,
one symposium of purpose

The Department of Mechanical Engineering

Established in 2009, the Department of Mechanical Engineering at GCE Bargur offers a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering backed by well-equipped, state-of-the-art laboratories. Core study areas span fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, heat transfer, solid mechanics, materials engineering, manufacturing, energy systems, dynamics & control systems, CAD and CIM.

Faculty research spans micro machining, IC engines, alternative fuels, nano coatings, composite materials, surface engineering, corrosion, optimisation, and refrigeration & air conditioning.

PHANTASM'27 — Vaagai 2k26

A national-level technical symposium designed to bring innovation, engineering excellence and creative problem-solving onto one platform. Rooted in the core principles of Mechanical Engineering, it is a convergence point for students, academicians and industry enthusiasts to take on real-world engineering challenges.

"To inspire a generation of engineers who innovate responsibly, think critically, and lead confidently in shaping the future of technology and industry."
